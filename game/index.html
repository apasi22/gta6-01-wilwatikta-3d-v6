Upgrade the car, repaint it, keep it alive. Bambang never asks where the car came from.

HOW TO PLAY

Drive: W/S accelerate & brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake. On foot: WASD walk, Shift run, E enter/exit the nearest car. Fight: J or click shoot, F punch, R reload, 1-4 weapons. Crew: Z Agus marks the route, X Bambang repairs, C Ratna clears your heat — each costs cash and needs a cooldown. Police: stars rise with heat; hide until the search ring fades, or ram the roadblocks. Maps & menus: M map, P pause, T radio station, B mute. Complete all 12 missions to own Wilwatikta.
